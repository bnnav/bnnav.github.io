Woman-owned live event AV crew

Services

LED Video Wall Techs & Leads

Build, map, configure and run LED walls, including NovaStar processor setup and operation, from load-in to strike.

V1 / Video Engineer

Signal flow, switching and routing, and show-calling support for the video department.

Engineer-in-Charge (EIC)

Technical leadership for the video system, coordinating crew, gear and schedule with production.

Resolume & Media Playback

Content playback, layering and live mixing in Resolume, plus playback operation for keynotes and general sessions.

LED Wall Rental + Crew Packages

LED wall rental bundled with our own crew, so one team handles the gear and the people.
